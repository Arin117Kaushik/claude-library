# Why this file exists: Whisper mishears "Claude" as "cloud" often enough to
# matter for anyone making or studying AI content. The rules below fix the
# unambiguous cases and deliberately leave genuine cloud-computing talk alone.
# Product names go first because they are never ambiguous; the standalone rule
# runs after and steps aside for "the cloud", "a cloud", and anything followed
# by a cloud-computing word.
#
# Add your own lines as you spot mishearings in your niche's vocabulary.
# Keep the tab between the two halves.
